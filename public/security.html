Security

Found a security problem in Boardo? Write to kaiser@kaiserkhan.com. Please give time to fix it before telling others, and use only your own account and boards to test.

Useful to include

  • What you found, and the steps that show it.
  • What someone could do with it.
  • The browser and device you used.

Please don't slow Boardo down for others, send bulk email through it, or look at data that isn't yours. There's no paid reward.

Keeping your account safe

  • Never share a sign-in code. Nobody from Boardo will ask for one.
  • Lost a device? On another one: Account, Sign out other devices.
  • Changing your email or deleting your account asks you to confirm it's you.

Boardo's code is public at github.com/qkonline/boardo.
