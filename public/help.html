Help

Stuck, or something isn't working? Write to kaiser@kaiserkhan.com. Say what you were doing and what you saw, and don't send sign-in codes.

Signing in

  • No code in your email? Check your spam folder, and that the address is right. You can ask for another after a minute.
  • Already have a code? Choose I already have a code. Each works for 10 minutes, and only the newest one works.
  • Invited to a board? Sign in with the address the invite was sent to. Invites expire after 14 days: ask whoever sent yours for a new one.
  • Lost access to your email? Sign in with a passkey if you've added one, then change your email in Account.

Boards

  • Offline: boards you've opened keep working, and your changes go out when you're back online.
  • A change was undone? A copy is in Account, under Undone changes.
  • An archived card: open the board's Archived link, then Restore.
  • A copy of a board: an admin can download it in Board settings, Leave or delete.
  • Deleted a board by mistake? Write within 14 days. Its text can usually be put back, but not its attachments.
